Revision plan Modules Practice routine

A 14-day revision plan

Days 1–3

Core coding foundations

Refresh complexity analysis, arrays, strings, hash maps, linked lists, stacks, and queues. Solve 2–3 timed questions daily and narrate your choices aloud.

Days 4–6

Patterns and problem solving

Work through two pointers, sliding window, binary search, recursion, trees, graphs, and dynamic programming. Focus on recognising the pattern before coding.

Days 7–9

System design

Practise requirements, estimates, APIs, storage, scaling, and trade-offs. Draw one design daily, then review its bottlenecks.

Days 10–11

Interview communication

Run mock answers with a timer. Improve clarification, structured reasoning, testing, and recovery when you get stuck.

Days 12–14

Full simulations

Complete one coding and one design mock per day. Review mistakes, revisit weak topics, and prepare questions for your interviewers.
